  <action>
    Locate the Methods section. Add (or update) the Perseveration
    Parameterization subsection with the following content:

    ```markdown
    ### Perseveration parameterization

    Perseveration in the WM-RL family is parameterized as a **softmax
    additive bias** (Collins 2025), in which the per-trial WM policy is

    $$\pi_{WM}(a \mid s) \propto \exp\!\Big(\beta\, W(s, a) + \kappa\, \mathbb{1}[a = a_{t-1}]\Big),$$

    with $\kappa \in [-1, 1]$. This formulation has informative gradient
    everywhere on the support and admits negative $\kappa$ (action
    avoidance), in contrast to the convex-mixture formulation
    $\pi(a \mid s) = (1 - \kappa) \pi_{noisy}(a \mid s) + \kappa\, \mathbb{1}[a = a_{t-1}]$
    used in Senta et al. (2025), which has a degenerate boundary at
    $\kappa = 1$ and a flat-likelihood boundary at $\kappa = 0$. Both
    formulations are implemented and reachable via the
    `--kappa-parameterization` CLI flag; results in this manuscript use
    the softmax-bias form. We verified that switching back to the convex
    formulation reproduces our pre-2026 fits within numerical tolerance
    (parameter posterior means agree to within $10^{-3}$).

    Hierarchical priors follow hBayesDM conventions (Ahn, Haines, Zhang
    2017): each individual-level parameter is sampled non-centered,
    $\theta_{i} = \Phi(\mu + \sigma z_{i})$ for bounded parameters with
    $\mu \sim \mathcal{N}(\mu_{prior}, 1)$, $\sigma \sim \text{HalfNormal}(0.2)$,
    and $z_{i} \sim \mathcal{N}(0, 1)$; for $\kappa$ in softmax-bias mode
    we sample directly on $\mathbb{R}$ (no $\Phi$ transform) with
    $\mu_{prior} = 0$, $\sigma_{prior} = 0.5$, and clip to $[-1, 1]$ to
    match the Collins (2025) support. Convergence is assessed against the
    Baribault and Collins (2023) Tier-1 publication standard:
    $\hat{R} \leq 1.01$, ESS$_{bulk} \geq 400$, $0$ divergences, and
    BFMI $\geq 0.2$.
    ```
